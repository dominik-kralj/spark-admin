Colour tokens

Most of the interface is white, gray.50 and text. Navy and blue are accents; green means OK and red means not OK. Ratios are WCAG 2.2 contrast against the two page surfaces. Text needs 4.5 : 1; icons, input borders and focus rings need 3 : 1.
