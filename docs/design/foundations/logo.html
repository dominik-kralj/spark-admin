Logo

A licence plate with its blue band and a check mark, set in a rounded tile: a vehicle that has been checked. Four flat shapes, no gradients, glows or 3D. The wordmark is IBM Plex Sans SemiBold with 6 % letter spacing.

  • The mark is drawn on a 32 unit grid. At 24 px the band is about 3 px wide and the check stroke about 1.7 px, so both stay visible.
  • In the single colour versions the band and the plate are cut out of the tile, so the mark works as one filled shape for stamps, engraving or a monochrome favicon.
  • In the app the lockup reads SPARK Admin, with Admin in regular weight and gray.600 after the wordmark.
  • Keep clear space of a quarter of the tile width around the mark. Do not add shadows, outlines, gradients or a car.
